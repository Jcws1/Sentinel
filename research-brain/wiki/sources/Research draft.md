# Research draft

Source: [unchanged DOCX snapshot](../../raw/inputs/Sentinel_Report_Research_draft.docx). Original: `D:\Downloads\Sentinel_Report_Research_draft.docx`.

The document has 235 top-level paragraphs and five embedded images. Its text and embedded comparison/risk tables were inspected. The images also include team details and external operational illustrations; those photographs are not evidence of Sentinel field trials.

The useful framing is a fleet-coordination and operator-capacity problem, an emphasis on shared mission information, and an intended transition from individual piloting to supervision. These are aims and problem framing, not measured human-factors outcomes.

The draft also describes autonomous planning, sensor fusion and confidence scoring, operation under navigation denial, and validated partner integrations. The current repository evidence does not establish these as demonstrated capabilities. Some sections, including technical approach and test methodology, remain empty or incomplete. The competitor and external conflict claims were not independently verified in this task and were not used in the abstract.

The [[wiki/sources/Sentinel v3 specification|Sentinel v3 specification]] is a useful control on this draft. Where the draft asserts sensor fusion and confidence scoring as capability, the specification places the same view among items that may remain partly conceptual, and defers advanced sensor-fusion visualisation outright. Where the draft implies a broad multi-domain product, the specification states that HADR is a future platform example and not a build target. The draft mixes achieved-sounding claims and future ambitions; for these selected features, the specification states the deferral more explicitly.

The draft's reference list is a research lead rather than a verified bibliography. Some entries are incomplete; their existence does not validate associated claims. See [[wiki/topics/Claims and gaps|Claims and gaps]] and [[wiki/topics/Verified scope|Verified scope]].

This document has since been superseded for submission purposes by
[[wiki/sources/Report draft V2|Report draft V2]], which is a different document
rather than a revision of this one. On the claims this vault can check, V2 drops
the overreaches recorded here: sensor fusion, confidence scoring, GNSS-denied
aircraft capability and the asserted partner integrations do not appear in it.
This page remains the record of what the earlier research document claimed, and
its problem framing and reference leads are still the origin of several sections
now carried into the report.
