# Submission requirements

Source: [unchanged PDF snapshot](../../raw/inputs/DVL_Final_Deliverables_Guidelines.pdf). Original: `D:\Downloads\DVL_Final_Deliverables_Guidelines (1).pdf`.

Report requirements in PDF section 2.1 on page 2 call for a 250-word cover abstract covering the problem, approach, headline quantitative result, and operational implication. They set a ten-page maximum for body content, not a required length, and specify an 11-point serif body, single spacing, PDF submission, references excluded from the body count, and optional appendices limited to five pages. IEEE reference style is recommended in the PDF. The inherited preference notes below record it as required for this report.

## Other recorded submission requirements

Pages 2-4 recommend covering prior work, technical decisions, methodology, results,
limitations, defence applications, dependencies, defensibility and incubation.

The PDF's section 2.2 sets these page budgets: a research and technical core of
about seven pages, being introduction and problem statement ~0.5, prior work and
competitive landscape ~0.5, technical approach ~3.5, test methodology ~1.5 and
results and discussion ~1; then strategy and transition of about three pages,
being defence applications and CONOPS ~1.0, defensibility ~0.5 and incubation
plan ~1.5, with incubation marked unscored. The introduction is asked for "a
short before-and-after -- what existed on 21 June versus what exists now".
Test methodology is asked for TRL entry and exit with the evidence behind the
exit claim, and to "name what you didn't test rather than omitting it".
See [[wiki/outputs/Report evidence map|Report evidence map]] for what this vault
can supply against each of those sections.
The methodology asks for conditions, baseline, trial count and untested degraded
conditions; results should include uncertainty and failure modes.

Page 4 also requires a video of at most two minutes, MP4 plus an unlisted backup
link, showing the system rather than a pitch or architecture walkthrough.
Simulated, rendered, staged or accelerated footage must be labelled when shown.
The prescribed filenames are `TeamName_FinalReport_v1.pdf` and
`TeamName_Demo.mp4`. The cover states a due date of 20 September without a year.

## Inherited user preferences

The prior note records these user/organiser updates. Original messages are not
snapshotted, so their provenance cannot be independently rechecked here. Retain
them as inherited report preferences, not PDF requirements: the organisers favour a concise report and do not require filling ten pages. They emphasise methodology, architecture, and decisions, explained for technically informed judges who are not all software developers. The requested template uses Times New Roman 11-point body text and native Word elements without graphics.

The prior note also records that the abstract request required evaluation conditions, the boundary between simulation and field evidence, baseline, trial count, uncertainty where available, and a closing practical implication and limitation. See [[wiki/outputs/Abstract|Abstract]].

## Judging criteria alongside submission format

The [judge scorecard](<../../raw/inputs/DVL 3 month track - Judge Scorecard.pdf>),
pages 1–8, adds independent capability (40%), mission-need (30%) and commercial
viability (30%) criteria. It accepts simulation results for C2 systems and both
live and video demonstrations. It does not cancel this guidelines PDF's separate
report and two-minute submitted-video requirements or its on-screen labelling
of simulated/rendered footage. The 20 September submission date and 21–27
September demo week concern different events; neither PDF specifies a year.
